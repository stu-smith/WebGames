How to Play

Neon Breakout

Neon Breakout is the classic brick-smasher, lit up. Slide your paddle to bounce the ball into the glowing wall above — clear every brick to advance. Every level is auto-generated and perfectly symmetrical, and each one packs smaller, tougher bricks and a faster ball.

Destroyed bricks often drop capsules. Catch them with your paddle — most are powerups, but the red-ringed ones will hurt you. Lose the ball off the bottom and you lose a life.

Powerups & Powerdowns

  • M — Multi-ball: every ball splits into three.
  • + — Extra ball: one more life in reserve.
  • L — Lasers: your paddle auto-fires for 10 seconds.
  • W — Wide paddle: much easier to keep the rally alive.
  • S — Slow-mo: the ball drops to a gentle cruise.
  • P — Power ball: smashes straight through bricks without bouncing.
  • E — Blast ball: every impact explodes, damaging nearby bricks.
  • − — Shrink: your paddle gets worryingly small.
  • F — Fast: the ball kicks into overdrive.

Controls

  • Mouse / TouchMove paddle
  • ← →Move paddle
  • Space / ClickLaunch ball

Tips

  • The ball's angle depends on where it hits the paddle — the edges send it out steep.
  • Steel bricks never break — even the power ball bounces off them.
  • Multi-ball plus lasers can strip a whole level in seconds.
  • Sometimes it's smarter to dodge a red capsule than to catch it.
