How to Play

Neon Flap

Neon Flap is a one-button arcade dodger. Your glowing orb constantly falls — flap to pulse upward and thread the gaps between the neon towers scrolling toward you.

Clip a tower, the ceiling grid or the ground and it's game over. Each tower you pass scores a point, and the towers speed up and tighten as your score climbs.

Controls

  • Space / ↑Flap
  • Click / TapFlap

Tips

  • Small, frequent taps are easier to control than big rescue flaps.
  • Aim for the centre of the gap — momentum carries you further than you think.
  • Watch the next gap, not the orb. Your eyes should always be one tower ahead.
