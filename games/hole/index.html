How to Play

Neon Hole

Neon Hole drops you into a glowing city as a hungry hole in the ground. Glide around the streets and everything smaller than you tumbles in — people, benches, cars, trees, and eventually entire skyscrapers. Every bite makes you bigger, and bigger means hungrier.

You're not alone: five rival holes are devouring the same city. A bigger hole can swallow a smaller one whole — eat your rivals for a huge bonus, but if one of them swallows you, you lose a life. Score the most points before the three-minute round ends.

The City

  • Suburbs — terraced streets, gardens and parked cars: perfect early snacks. Terraces come apart one house at a time.
  • The Park — packed with people, benches, railings and a fountain.
  • Market Quarter — shops, stalls and kiosks in tasty clusters.
  • Industrial Yard — containers, tanks and warehouses for mid-game growth.
  • Downtown — neon towers and skyscrapers. Only a true City Ender can eat these.
  • There's also a stadium, a church with a graveyard, and buses on the roads. It's all edible.

Controls

  • WASD / ←↑↓→Move
  • Mouse / TouchHold to steer toward the pointer
  • PPause
  • MMute

Tips

  • Your hole grows in whole-metre jumps — the bar under your size shows how close the next one is.
  • Round buildings (silos, water towers, the round tower) slip into a smaller hole than square ones — no corners to catch.
  • Big office blocks are built from separate slabs; eat them chunk by chunk.
  • Chain quick snacks to build a combo multiplier — up to ×4 points.
  • Check the minimap: dense clusters of dots mean dense calories.
  • Steer clear of bigger rivals — the leaderboard shows who's ahead, the label shows who's bigger.
  • Eating a rival steals a big chunk of their size and score.
  • After being swallowed you respawn smaller but briefly invincible — use it to escape.
