How to Play

Snake Arena

Snake Arena is a modern multiplayer-style twist on the classic Snake game. You share a huge neon arena with AI-controlled rival snakes. Glide around eating glowing orbs to grow longer and climb the leaderboard.

Cut in front of another snake so its head runs into your body and it crashes — then feast on the trail of orbs it leaves behind. But be careful: if your head hits any snake, you're eaten and it's game over.

Controls

  • Move mouseSteer your snake's head
  • ClickHold to boost speed
  • SpaceHold to boost speed

Tips

  • Boosting burns length — only sprint when it counts.
  • Encircle smaller snakes to trap them into crashing.
  • Stay near the edges early on to avoid crowded pile-ups.
