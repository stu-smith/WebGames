How to Play

Sudoku

Sudoku is a number-placement puzzle on a 9×9 grid. Fill every empty square so that each row, each column and each of the nine 3×3 boxes contains the digits 1 to 9 exactly once.

The clues you start with are fixed. Every puzzle here is generated with exactly one solution, so you never have to guess blindly — there is always a logical next step.

Difficulty

Each level caps how hard the reasoning can get, so a puzzle is never tougher than the label promises. The Technique readout shows the hardest step the grid in front of you actually needs.

  • Easy — around 40 clues, and every step is a single candidate.
  • Medium — fewer clues; at worst you need locked candidates.
  • Hard — at worst you need naked or hidden pairs, triples and quads.
  • Expert — minimal clues, and reasoning beyond the standard patterns.

Controls

  • ClickSelect a square
  • 1–9Enter a digit (again to clear it)
  • ←↑↓→Move the selection
  • ⌫Erase the square
  • NToggle pencil marks
  • UUndo
  • HReveal one square

Tips

  • Scan for a digit that has only one legal home left in a row, column or box.
  • Use pencil marks to park the options for a square before committing.
  • Best times are only kept for grids solved with no mistakes and no hints.
