How to Play

Wordle

Wordle is a daily word-guessing game. You have six tries to guess a hidden five-letter word. Type any valid five-letter word and press Enter to submit each guess.

After every guess the tiles change colour to show how close you were:

  • Green — the letter is correct and in the right spot.
  • Yellow — the letter is in the word but the wrong spot.
  • Grey — the letter is not in the word at all.

Use the clues to narrow down the answer and solve the puzzle before you run out of guesses.

Controls

  • TypeEnter letters (keyboard or on-screen)
  • EnterSubmit your guess
  • ⌫Delete the last letter
  • NewStart a fresh puzzle

Tips

  • Open with a word rich in vowels and common letters, like ADIEU or RAISE.
  • Watch the on-screen keyboard colours to rule out letters fast.
  • Don't waste guesses on letters you've already marked grey.
